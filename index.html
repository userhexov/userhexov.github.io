Brand History

From a small roadside kitchen to the whole world

Kentucky Fried Chicken was founded by Harland David Sanders, who is better known as the Colonel. He is a self-made man who achieved his greatest succes at the age of 65 years old. His story is one of hard work perseverance and a secret blend of 11 herbs and spices that nobody else know.

Sanders started with a modest gas station restaurant in Corbin, Kentucky. There he developed his unique pressure-frying method, that sealed in flavour and made his fried chicken unlike anything on the market at that time. The chain now operates in more then 150 countries across the globe.

In 1964 he sold the company for two million dollars, but his image stayed the face of KFC forever. Today the brand is one of the most recognisable fast food names in the world and it's still growing every year.
